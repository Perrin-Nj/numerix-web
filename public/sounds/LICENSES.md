# Sound licenses

Sounds marked "synthesized" are generated from scratch by `scripts/gen-sounds.mjs` (no samples) and dedicated to the public domain (CC0). The others are derived from "Interface Sounds" by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain). Original license text: `KENNEY_LICENSE.txt`. Files were converted to mono, WebM/Opus (32 kbps) and MP3 (64 kbps) with ffmpeg. New variants were also normalized (loudnorm I=-20, limiter 0.8) and capped at 1.1 s.
