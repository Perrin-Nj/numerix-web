# Numerix level guide, grades 1 to 4

Purpose: a plain level guide so our own original, story-driven questions land at the right difficulty. It is not a copy of any curriculum. Questions, stories and exercises are written by us.

## 1. What a child can handle, per grade

| | Grade 1 (6-7 y) | Grade 2 (7-8 y) | Grade 3 (8-9 y) | Grade 4 (9-10 y) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Counting and reading numbers | count to 100, skip by 2, 5, 10, groups of ten | read, write, order to 1 000 | to 10 000 | to 100 000 |
| Add / subtract | within 20, sums by decomposing; story situations | facts to 10+10 solid by now; mental within 100; with materials to 1 000 | all addition facts solid; written add/sub 3-4 digits begins | written add/sub up to 4 digits, with carrying |
| Multiply / divide | share and group with objects, repeated adding (no symbols pushed) | equal groups, arrays, doubling; still pictures | tables begin (2, 3, 4, 5, 10 first), sharing and grouping stories | all tables to 10x10 practised (full mastery comes later), division with remainder, 3-digit by 1-digit with own methods |
| Fractions | half and quarter in daily life, pictures | same, plus thirds in pictures | name and read fractions of a whole or group, compare simple ones | compare, order same denominator, equal fractions, link to decimals |
| Decimals, money | coins as counting objects | coins, totals to 100 cents | tenths first, prices | tenths and hundredths, add/subtract prices, change |
| Measure | compare lengths, cm and m, time on the hour and half hour | cm, dm, m with ruler; clock to 5 minutes; units of time | perimeter, mm, litres and mL, g and kg, time spans | area by counting squares, perimeter, unit relations |
| Geometry | place things on a grid, name flat shapes and solids | paths on a grid, congruent shapes, friezes | describe shapes by sides and corners | parallel and perpendicular, reflection, frieze and tiling |
| Data and chance | sort, pictograph, bar chart, certain/possible/impossible | read and make bar charts, count outcomes | read tables and line graphs begins, which is more likely | line graph, simulate chance, count outcomes with a table |

Mastery landmarks (likely): addition facts settled by end of grade 3, multiplication facts only start in grade 3 and are not fully settled until about grade 5. So grade 3-4 multiplication must stay gentle and scaffolded.

## 3. Reading limits (voice read-aloud on at all grades; text never carries the maths alone)

| Grade | Prompt (the question) | Story (setup) | Sentence length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | up to 10 words | up to 20 words total, 1-2 sentences | up to 8 words | icons plus audio; numerals big; no negatives, no multi-step |
| 2 | up to 15 words | up to 35 words, 2-3 sentences | up to 12 words | one idea per sentence; words like "more", "fewer", "in all" |
| 3 | up to 25 words | up to 60 words, 3-4 sentences | up to 16 words | one extra, unused number allowed from band 4 |
| 4 | up to 35 words | up to 90 words, 4-5 sentences | up to 20 words | two-step story allowed; light text tables allowed |

French and English must meet the same limits (French runs about 15 percent longer, so write French first, then check the cap on both).

## 4. Story shapes by grade (our own framing)

- Grade 1: add something, take something away, put two groups together, who has more. One step.
- Grade 2: the above plus missing part ("how many more to reach"), compare ("how many more than"). One step, sometimes a two-part answer.
- Grade 3: equal groups, sharing fairly, rows and columns. One step, rarely two.
- Grade 4: equal groups with leftovers, change from a purchase, comparing two measures. Two steps from band 4.

## 5. Difficulty bands (1 to 5) inside a grade

| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | warm-up, small numbers, pictures shown, 2-3 answer choices |
| 2 | still supported, medium numbers, 3-4 choices |
| 3 | grade standard, no picture help, typed or dragged answer |
| 4 | stretch, top of the grade range, an extra step or distractor |
| 5 | challenge, only offered after 3 clean sessions at band 4 |

## 6. ARI-OPE rule: drills only in grades 1 and 2
